# TherapyFlow  
**AI-Powered Clinical Practice & Patient Engagement Platform**  
_For Psychologists & Clients_

---

## 📖 Overview
TherapyFlow is an intelligent platform designed to support psychologists in managing clinical practice while empowering patients with personalized wellness tools. Unlike traditional therapy apps that only store data, TherapyFlow transforms information into actionable insights through its AI Analysis Engine — improving therapy outcomes and patient engagement.

---

## 🎯 Core Guidelines

### 1. Client Management Hub
- Maintain complete digital patient records:
  - Profiles, emergency contacts, medical & psychological history
  - Treatment plans, consent forms, session history
  - Secure document storage & referral tracking
- **AI Requirements:**
  - Summarize client history automatically
  - Generate quick overviews before sessions
  - Detect recurring concerns across multiple sessions

---

### 2. AI Session Workspace
- Provide a unified workspace during therapy sessions:
  - Note-taking, voice-to-text transcription, session timeline
  - Reference previous sessions & track treatment plans
- **AI Requirements:**
  - Generate SOAP notes & progress summaries
  - Highlight key discussion themes
  - Suggest follow-up questions
  - Identify missing clinical information

---

### 3. AI Session Preparation Brief
- Auto-generate pre-session briefs including:
  - Previous session summary, mood trends, journal insights
  - Assessment score changes, follow-up items, attendance history
- **AI Requirements:**
  - Deliver concise, actionable preparation notes
  - Highlight focus areas and suggested discussion topics

---

### 4. Intelligent Assessment Center
- Support standardized assessments:
  - PHQ-9, GAD-7, Burnout, Stress, Wellness Check-ins
- **AI Requirements:**
  - Automate scoring & trend analysis
  - Visualize progress & monitor risks
  - Generate clinical reports

---

### 5. Treatment Progress Dashboard
- Visualize therapy effectiveness:
  - Mood improvement, anxiety reduction, depression scores
  - Attendance consistency, goal completion, journal engagement
- **AI Requirements:**
  - Detect setbacks & highlight improvements
  - Identify behavioral patterns
  - Generate monthly progress reports

---

### 6. Practice Administration Assistant
- Automate routine administrative tasks:
  - Scheduling, reminders, forms, waiting list management
- **AI Requirements:**
  - Draft appointment messages & professional emails
  - Send automated reminders
  - Create structured follow-up plans

---

### 7. Secure Communication Center
- Enable safe therapist-client communication:
  - Messaging, file sharing, resource distribution
- **AI Requirements:**
  - Organize conversations
  - Summarize communication threads
  - Flag unanswered client concerns

---

## 👩‍⚕️ For Clients

### 1. Personal Wellness Dashboard
- Display mood, appointments, goals, resources, and progress.

### 2. Mood Tracking System
- Daily emotional check-ins (stress, anxiety, mood, energy, sleep).
- **AI Requirements:** Detect patterns (e.g., stress before meetings, sleep linked to anxiety).

### 3. Guided Reflection Journal
- Support text, voice, and photo entries.
- **AI Requirements:** Identify emotional themes, stressors, coping activities, growth indicators.

### 4. Assessment Portal
- Allow remote completion of questionnaires.
- Reduce paperwork before sessions.

### 5. Appointment & Scheduling Hub
- Book, reschedule, join online sessions, view reminders.

### 6. AI Wellness Companion
- Encourage journaling, track feelings, recommend therapist-approved resources.
- **Note:** Not a therapist or diagnostic tool.

### 7. Personalized Resource Library
- Curated by clinicians (e.g., Dr. Nyawose).
- **AI Requirements:** Recommend resources based on mood trends, therapy goals, journal themes, and assessments.
